# P-09 The generated OpenAPI drops the item bounds and the 409 of startOrderCheckout

status: accepted (overlay + generator fix)
found by: zenwave-tasks-from-code-and-models (board of baseline 2), 2026-11-17; revised after the OpenAPI was regenerated
flow: place-order-flow
blocks: none (T-74 follows the decision)

## Problem
The ZDL bounds the order lines: `quantity Integer required min(1)` and `unitPrice BigDecimal required min(0)`
([orders-checkout-domain-model.zdl:35-36](../../../arcadia-editions-modulith-api/orders-checkout-domain-model.zdl#L35),
[:68-69](../../../arcadia-editions-modulith-api/orders-checkout-domain-model.zdl#L68)), and `startOrderCheckout`
answers 409 on `StockUnavailable` (P-03 A, [:95-99](../../../arcadia-editions-modulith-api/orders-checkout-domain-model.zdl#L95)).
The OpenAPI regenerated from that ZDL on 2026-11-17 has none of it: `OrderItemInput.quantity` and `.unitPrice` carry
no `minimum`, and the operation lists only 201, with no 400, no 409 and no `StockUnavailable` schema. The 409 survives
only in the description text.

The earlier version of this proposal compared the ZDL `min(0)` (0 allowed) with a hand-written
`exclusiveMinimum: true` (0 refused). That hand edit is gone with the regeneration, so the bound conflict is resolved
in favour of the ZDL; what remains is a contract that says less than the model.

## Evidence
- `git diff orders-checkout-openapi.yml` in `arcadia-editions-modulith-api` (uncommitted): removes `minimum: 1`,
  `minimum: 0` / `exclusiveMinimum: true`, the 400 and 409 responses and the `StockUnavailable` /
  `StockUnavailableItem` schemas.
- The service side already follows the ZDL: `OrderItemInput` has `@Min(1)` and `@Min(0)` (0.10.0 regeneration), and
  `OrdersCheckoutApiExceptionHandler` answers 409 with its own body (P-03 A).
- The controller interface is generated from the OpenAPI, so request validation at the web layer sees no bounds.

## Options
- **A.** The ZDL is the contract; the generated OpenAPI stays as generated. The web layer validates by mapping to
  `OrderItemInput` (`@Valid`, the ZDL bounds), and the 409 body stays the hand-written one. Report the generator gaps
  (`min` → `minimum`, `withEvents` alternatives → error responses) to ZenWave SDK and list them in the known generator
  issues.
- **B.** Keep a hand-maintained patch on top of the generated OpenAPI (bounds, 400, 409, `StockUnavailable` schemas),
  reapplied after every regeneration and checked by the staleness check.
- **C.** Stop generating this OpenAPI: it becomes hand-written, the source of truth for the web contract.

## Recommendation
A: one source of truth (the ZDL), no patch to forget after the next regeneration; clients lose only the documented
bounds and 409 until the generator emits them. 0 stays a valid price (ZDL `min(0)`).

## Decision
Decided by the person on 2026-11-17. The OpenAPI stays generated from the ZDL:
- the 400 and 409 responses and the `StockUnavailable` / `StockUnavailableItem` schemas come from
  `orders-checkout-openapi-overlay.yml`, next to the ZDL, applied by `ZDLToOpenAPIPlugin` (`openapiOverlayFiles`);
- the `minimum` bounds come from the ZDL `min(...)` once ZenWave SDK emits them for 0 (fix in `zdl-to-openapi`).
0 is a valid price (ZDL `min(0)`). T-74 validates against the ZDL bounds.
